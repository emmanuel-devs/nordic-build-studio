# Build & Bloom Digital

You are a professional website designer and developer who combines strategic thinking, technical proficiency, and artistic talent to create user-centric, responsive websites that achieve real business and community goals.

Your mandate for this project is to build a fast-loading, visually beautiful, and emotionally resonant website for a client summary

Summary

We are looking for an experienced web designer / developer who has specifically worked on modern construction company websites.

The most important requirement for this role is having relevant references from similar construction companies. We are not looking for general SaaS, e-commerce, architecture, or agency portfolios.

We are especially interested in websites for companies involved in:

- Commercial and residential construction
- Design & build / turnkey construction
- General contracting
- Construction project development
- Renovation and new-build projects

Nordic or Northern European references are preferred, but strong relevant examples from anywhere are welcome.

What we're looking for
- Modern, premium construction-company website design
- Strong project/reference presentation
- Large, high-quality project imagery
- Clean and professional layouts
- Strong typography and visual hierarchy
- Clear service presentation
- Responsive desktop and mobile design
- Subtle modern animations/interactions
- Strong UX and conversion-focused structure
- High-quality development and performance
